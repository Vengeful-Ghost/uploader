Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

HistoryToldShorter Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel HistoryToldShorter. It uses the YouTube API Services.

What data the app accesses

  • Permission to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (youtube.upload) and to read that channel's basic information (youtube.readonly).
  • The app uploads videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails that the channel owner created.

How data is stored and shared

  • The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and in the owner's private automation environment. They are used only to upload and schedule the owner's videos.
  • The app does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and it does not access any other user's data.

Google and YouTube

By using YouTube features through this app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access at any time at Google security settings.
